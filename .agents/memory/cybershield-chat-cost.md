Keep the app chat-only: no FAQ page or right-side information cards. Use a broad, curated offline cybersecurity-awareness library rather than silently adding live AI.

**Why:** The user asked to remove the FAQ and right-side cards, and to remove UI wording that reveals the chat is not live AI. They chose offline answers after learning live AI usage would be billed to their Replit credits; they want the tool free for students. They later chose to expand the offline cybersecurity guidance beyond the original four scenarios.

**How to apply:** Preserve the chat-only, no-sign-up, no-AI-usage intent. Do not label answers as prepared or say they are not live AI. Keep expanding common cybersecurity-awareness coverage with a clear fallback for questions that do not match; do not claim the app can answer literally every question. Before adding pages, right-side cards, or a live model, explain the change and any creator-side usage charges, then get the user's approval.
