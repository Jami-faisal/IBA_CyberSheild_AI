Keep the app chat-only: no FAQ page or right-side information cards. Keep the student chat on prepared answers rather than silently adding live AI.

**Why:** The user asked to remove the FAQ and right-side cards, and to remove UI wording that reveals the chat is not live AI. They chose prepared answers after learning that live AI usage would be billed to their Replit credits; they want the tool free for students.

**How to apply:** Preserve the chat-only, no-sign-up, no-AI-usage intent. Do not label answers as prepared or say they are not live AI, but keep the four-topic limit clear and do not claim live AI. Before adding pages, right-side cards, or a live model, explain the change and any creator-side usage charges, then get the user's approval.
